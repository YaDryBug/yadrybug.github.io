> ## Ingredients
>
> - 2 large chicken breasts, butterflied
> - 100g plain flour
> - 2 eggs, beaten
> - 150g panko breadcrumbs
> - 1 onion, thinly sliced
> - 2 carrots, roughly chopped
> - 3 garlic cloves, grated
> - 1 thumb of ginger, grated
> - 2 tbsp mild curry powder
> - 1 tbsp plain flour (for the sauce)
> - 500ml chicken stock
> - 1 tbsp soy sauce
> - 1 tbsp honey
> - 1 bay leaf
> - Vegetable oil, for frying
> - Cooked rice, to serve
## Method
1. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a saucepan over a medium heat. Add the onion and carrots and cook for 8–10 minutes until softened.
2. Add the garlic, ginger and curry powder, and cook for a further minute until fragrant.
3. Stir in the plain flour and cook for 1 minute, then gradually whisk in the chicken stock to avoid lumps. Add the soy sauce, honey and bay leaf.
4. Simmer the sauce for 15–20 minutes until thickened, then blitz until smooth using a hand blender. Season to taste and keep warm.
5. While the sauce simmers, set up three shallow bowls: one with flour, one with beaten egg, and one with panko breadcrumbs.
6. Coat each chicken breast first in flour, then egg, then press firmly into the panko to coat.
7. Heat a generous layer of vegetable oil in a frying pan over a medium-high heat. Fry the chicken for 4–5 minutes on each side until golden and cooked through.
8. Rest the chicken for a few minutes, then slice.
9. Serve the sliced chicken over rice, generously topped with the curry sauce.
